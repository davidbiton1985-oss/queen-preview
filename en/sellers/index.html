The process

Five steps, one partner

You always know what is happening and what the next step is. The partner who meets you at home is the one who stays with you to the signing.

Uncompromising professionalism, sensitivity and care for people, integrity and fairness.

In the office’s own words
  2. 01

    A visit and a price estimate

    We come to you, get to know the home and the area, and give you a price estimate. Every home has its own story and its own complexity, and that is where we start.

    • A visit to the property
    • A price estimate (not a formal appraisal)
  3. 02

    Preparing the home

    The home should look its best before the first buyer ever walks in.

    • Home staging where it helps
    • Professional photography: stills
    • Video
    • Drone footage
  4. 03

    Marketing and finding buyers

    Distinctive marketing across channels, with a digital agency responsible for marketing our listings, and a search for the right buyers.

    • Distinctive marketing
    • Marketing across channels
    • Finding potential buyers
    • Returning residents from around the world, through the "Home for Returning Residents" community

    Negotiation

    Professional negotiation, until the deal is tailored precisely to your needs.

    • Professional negotiation
  6. 05

    To signing, and well beyond

    Personal support with professionals chosen with care, to the office’s own standards. Until the contract is signed, and well beyond… we are in it together, hand in hand.
