Who we are

Hila Druker and Shimrit Shalom

Queen is a boutique brokerage in the commercial centre of Neve Zemer, Ra'anana.

We specialise in selling apartments and private houses in Ra'anana and the surrounding area, in families moving up, and in back-to-back deals tailored precisely to what our clients need. Hila and Shimrit, the owners, bring long experience in brokerage, marketing and real estate.

Buying or selling a home is one of the most important moments in life. Every home has its own story and its own complexity, so we walk every client through each step: with professionalism, sensitivity, integrity and fairness.

How we work

From the first visit to the signing

  1. 01

    A visit and a price assessment

    We meet you at home, get to know the property and its story, and give a price assessment.

  2. 02

    Preparing the home for sale

    Home staging where needed, and professional photography: stills, video and drone.

  3. 03

    Marketing and finding buyers

    Tailored marketing, and finding the potential buyers who suit the home.

  4. 04

    Negotiation

    Professional negotiation with every party, until the deal closes the right way.

  5. 05

    Working with professionals

    Lawyers, mortgage advisers, renovation contractors and architects, carefully chosen to the office’s standards.

  6. 06

    Up to the signing, and well beyond

    We are with you up to the signing of the contract, and after it too.

What we specialise in

Moving up, back to back

Selling your current home and buying the next one? We coordinate the two deals so the move happens on time, without the pressure.

Returning residents and new immigrants

Many of our clients come from around the world, referred by the founder and manager of the "Home for Returning Residents" community. We work with them in Hebrew and in English.

A property deal is done with professionals. In our case, two professional women. We are always one step ahead.
