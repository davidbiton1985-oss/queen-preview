How we work

Five steps. The same two partners through all of them.

  1. 1

    Price estimate

    A visit to the home and an analysis of real transactions in the neighbourhood. Free, no obligation.

    Start with a price estimate
  2. 2

    Preparation

    Light tidying and styling, small repairs and professional photography.

  3. 3

    Targeted marketing

    Direct contact with relevant buyers, plus the portals and social channels.

  4. 4

    Negotiation

    We run the conversation with the other side on the basis of sales data, until there is an agreement that works for you.

  5. 5

    Signing and handover

    Coordinating the lawyers, the mortgage and the dates, until the home passes to its new owners.
