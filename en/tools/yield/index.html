An estimate only

Gross yield = the full annual rent divided by the purchase price. Net yield = the rent actually collected minus expenses, divided by the price plus purchase costs. The calculation is before tax and without financing (mortgage) costs, and ignores any change in the property’s value. This is an estimate only and is not financial, investment or tax advice.
